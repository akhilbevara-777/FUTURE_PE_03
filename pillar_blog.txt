# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Which is the best salon in Bangalore for bridal makeup?

The best bridal salon depends on your budget, makeup style preference, and customer reviews. Look for salons with experienced bridal makeup artists and verified portfolios.

## How much does a hair spa cost in Bangalore?

Hair spa services generally range from ₹800 to ₹3000 depending on hair length, products used, and salon reputation.

## Which areas in Bangalore have top-rated salons?

Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala, HSR Layout, JP Nagar, and Jayanagar are known for premium salons and beauty studios.

## How often should I visit a salon?

Most experts recommend visiting a salon every 4–6 weeks for hair maintenance and monthly for skincare treatments.

## Are salon treatments safe for sensitive skin?

Professional salons usually conduct skin analysis before treatments. Always inform the beautician about allergies or skin sensitivity.
